FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

What is the difference between Ex-Showroom and On-Road price?
Ex-showroom price includes factory cost and GST. On-road price additionally includes RTO registration, road tax, mandatory insurance (comprehensive + third party), and TCS as applicable in your state in India.
Are BMW Electric vehicles eligible for subsidies in India?
Yes, several state governments offer road tax exemptions and registration waivers for battery electric vehicles (BEVs) like the BMW iX, i7, i4, and iX1.
What warranty and maintenance packages come with BMW Elite cars?
All new BMW cars come with a standard 2-year unlimited kilometers warranty, extendable up to 5 years. BMW Repair Inclusive and Service Inclusive packages are available for hassle-free ownership.
How do I book a personalized test drive?
You can fill out our online Test Drive booking form on this website, select your preferred model and dealership city, and our concierge team will finalize your appointment within 2 hours.
